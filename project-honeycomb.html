Cardboard Honeycomb Pattern Cutting Machine

Overview

Currently underway as part of the Semester 5 ME3201 Machine Design Project, this ongoing endeavor focuses on developing a sustainable, motor-powered alternative to expensive industrial honeycomb paper machines. The design centers around integrating a custom sliding-mesh gearbox with a highly efficient 0.5 HP AC induction motor to make eco-friendly packaging production accessible, affordable, and easy to maintain for local small businesses.


The Challenge

The global e-commerce boom has resulted in a massive increase in plastic packaging waste. While honeycomb paper crafted from waste corrugated cardboard is an excellent, biodegradable alternative, the industrial electric machines required to manufacture it carry prohibitive capital costs ranging from $2,000 to $4,500. Furthermore, running heavy-duty industrial motors is economically unviable in Sri Lanka due to high electricity tariffs. Modern imported machines also rely on complex, fragile electronics (such as VFDs and PLCs) for speed and torque reduction. A single electronic failure results in prolonged downtime and forces reliance on specialized technicians and expensive imported spare parts.


Mechanical Design & Gearbox Integration

To overcome these localized hurdles without relying on expensive electronics, the project utilizes a purely mechanical drivetrain. A low-power, highly efficient 0.5 HP (370W) electric motor is paired with a custom sliding-mesh gearbox. After an initial 6:1 chain-drive reduction brings the input speed to 160 RPM, the mechanical transmission actively multiplies torque. This ensures sufficient shearing force is generated to cut the internal flutes of standard corrugated cardboard without causing motor overload or blade binding.


System Parameters & Kinematics

The machine's gearbox features dual-direction rotation with three forward gears and one reverse gear, specifically mapped to adapt the mechanical advantage for varying cardboard flute thicknesses:


  • 1st Gear (Ratio 2:1 | 44 Nm Torque): Provides maximum shearing power at a lower feed rate (12 m/min) to process thick, heavy-duty BC-flute cardboard.
  • 2nd Gear (Ratio 1.3:1 | 28 Nm Torque): Delivers a balanced mix of speed (24 m/min) and power optimized for processing standard C-flute corrugated shipping boxes.
  • 3rd Gear (Ratio 1:1 | 22 Nm Torque): Functions as a direct drive utilizing a 4-tooth square dog clutch for the high-speed processing (30 m/min) of thinner E-flute materials where extreme torque multiplication is unnecessary.
  • Reverse Gear (Ratio 1.3:1 | 28 Nm Torque): Provides substantial torque in the opposite direction, which is critical for executing custom "half-cuts" and safely backing out wedged cardboard to clear severe jams.

Key Contribution

Spearheaded the comprehensive 3D CAD modeling of the entire machine assembly and executed core mechanical calculations. This included rigorous shear and crushing stress analysis for transmission keys and splines to guarantee safe torque transfer, kinematic design of the manual gear-shifting linkage, and dynamic load evaluations to correctly specify deep groove ball bearings for a target 5,840-hour service life.
