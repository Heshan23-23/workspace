The Pedal-Powered Cardboard Honeycomb Machine

Overview

Currently underway as part of the Semester 5 ME3201 Machine Design Project, this ongoing endeavor focuses on developing a sustainable, human-powered alternative to industrial honeycomb paper machines. The design centers around integrating a custom mechanical gearbox into a pedal-powered architecture to make eco-friendly packaging production accessible and affordable.


The Challenge

The global e-commerce boom has resulted in a 30% increase in plastic packaging waste. While honeycomb paper crafted from waste corrugated cardboard is an excellent, shock-absorbing sustainable alternative, the industrial electric machines required to manufacture it are massive and highly expensive, costing between $2,000 and $4,500. This high capital expenditure prevents small businesses and eco-startups from adopting the technology. Furthermore, a simple human-powered design is unviable because average human leg output (around 80Nm) is completely insufficient to crush and shear 3-ply corrugated cardboard, leading to immediate mechanical stall-out.


Mechanical Design & Gearbox Integration

To eliminate this torque limitation, the project utilizes a custom sliding mesh gearbox. This mechanical transmission allows a human operator to maintain a comfortable, consistent pedaling cadence of 60 RPM, generating between 100 and 150 Watts of power. The gearbox actively trades rotational speed for a massive increase in shearing force, preventing the system from stalling under a heavy load.


System Parameters & Kinematics

The machine's sliding mesh gearbox features dual-direction rotation with three forward gears and one reverse gear, specifically mapped to adapt the mechanical advantage for varying board thicknesses:


  • 1st Gear (15 RPM target speed): Provides maximum crushing power to process very thick, triple-wall appliance boxes.
  • 2nd Gear (25 RPM target speed): Delivers a balanced mix of speed and power suited for processing standard corrugated shipping boxes.
  • 3rd Gear (60 RPM target speed): Functions as a 1:1 direct drive for the high-speed processing of lightweight materials and thin Kraft paper where torque multiplication is unnecessary.
  • Reverse Gear: Designed to output torque greater than the 3rd forward gear, which is critical for executing custom "half-cuts," safely ripping out wedged cardboard to clear severe jams, and performing routine maintenance without dismantling the unit.

Key Contribution

Designed the mechanical gearbox and output rollers, and calculated the required power and gear ratios needed to easily crush thick cardboard.
